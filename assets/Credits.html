In tribute to Alain Roy’s Pathways Into Cheating.

An independent, unofficial save editor for the modern macOS port of Pathways Into Darkness.

Thanks to Micah Anderson, Ron Hunsinger, Loren Petrich, Ben Semmler, and the Pathways community for documenting the original formats.

Developed by ChatGPT (OpenAI), using Codex. Idea, project direction and in-game testing by Jean Savoye. Original icon generated with OpenAI image generation.
